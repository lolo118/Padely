# ChampionCard

The one glorified surface in the product: the winners of a cuadro, on the public tournament page and on the player's profile. Black and gold, in both themes (`data-theme="dark"`): `black` ground with a faint `gold-deep` glow, the double gold frame (`gold` outside, `gold-deep` inside), `radius-xl`, and the ball turned to gold — metallic, `gold-light` to `gold-deep` — bleeding off the top-right corner.

- Names in `display-lg` (Barlow Condensed 800), uppercase, filled with the gold gradient; the trophy and the cuadro in a `gold` eyebrow with wide tracking; tournament, club and date in `on-night-muted`.
- A thin gold rule separates the runners-up, who get one line in silver (`on-night-muted`) with a muted ball; nothing else competes with the champions.
- One per cuadro; a tournament with three cuadros shows three cards in a column, never a carousel.
- This is the only place an exclamation mark may appear ("¡Campeones!") and the only place gold appears outside the final match card. Gold is never a button and never sits on a Día surface.
- Share from here: the card is the image the player posts, so it must read at 320px wide.
